# FP-08+09 requirements: work encounters, acceptance and the XP ledger

One session, one design, one plan, three PRs (batching approved by Eric on 2026-11-15, limits in the program context §3). PR-A is the spec, contracts, store and policy, PR-B the service, routes and docs, PR-C the UI. Requirement ids are registered in `spec/ids.md`. Decisions are recorded in `design.md`.

Under program amendment PA-1, read every "agent" below that anchors a binding, a contributor, XP or a level as bot. Bot goals, key results and the work ledger are denied to apps (`docs/compatibility/monitoring-scope.md`), so no encounter is built from them. The only inputs are the attempts FP-04 projects (`bot-turn` and `worker`) and what the app owner types. Tokens are never XP (FD-29). Where a criterion mentions a later feature, it defines a contract now and the full integration assertion lands with the owning feature.

## Acceptance criteria (verbatim from the intake)

- **FP-08-AC01:** A session ending without accepted evidence does not defeat a work enemy or become reward eligible.
- **FP-08-AC02:** A real task can be linked to an encounter and accepted via an attributable human decision or approved verifier tied to its artifact revision.
  - Reading (Q3, 2026-11-18): there is no automatic verifier in this POC. The human path is the whole of AC02: the decision records the actor `app-owner`, the time and the evidence revision the reviewer echoed back, and a changed revision is refused as `stale_evidence`. A task is "linked" by listing its attempt ids on a WorkItem.
- **FP-08-AC03:** Retries do not create fresh independent objectives; a concurrent multi-agent objective has one consistent outcome.
- **FP-08-AC04:** Rejected, cancelled, blocked, awaiting-review, accepted, and reopened cases have tested transitions and audit evidence.
- **FP-08-AC05:** Unknown milestone size remains unknown; visual progress cannot be inferred from token count or animation duration.
- **FP-08-AC06:** Unauthorized or stale acceptance is rejected, and contributor identities are inspectable rather than invented by a summarizer.
  - Reading (Q3): the host gives an app no per-user identity, so "unauthorized" means a request the signed proxy did not vouch for, or one that reaches acceptance by any path other than the signed decision route. The only actor is the constant `app-owner`.
- **FP-09-AC01:** Concurrent acceptance requests and duplicate/replayed observations yield one net award for the same eligible work.
- **FP-09-AC02:** Property-based tests prove reward-budget conservation, deterministic allocation, and no duplicate credit across retries and parent/child structures.
  - Reading (Q11): the properties are checked with seeded randomized tests from the standard library (`random.Random(seed)`) over generated work trees, contributor sets and operation sequences, plus real thread concurrency. No property-testing package is added.
- **FP-09-AC03:** Reopen/reaccept/correct flows preserve auditable history and cannot farm additional bounty for unchanged work.
- **FP-09-AC04:** Identity rename/reroll/rebinding workflows obey the approved ownership rule and never mint or silently lose credit.
  - Reading (Q7): the ownership rule is that XP belongs to the bot binding (`bot_binding.binding_id`) and to nothing else. Rename, reroll, portrait change and rebinding notes write only Specter rows, which the ledger never reads.
- **FP-09-AC05:** A displayed level is traceable to ledger entries and policy version; it does not imply improved model ability or current health.
- **FP-09-AC06:** Summoning/asset-generation/self-monitoring maintenance tasks are excluded from normal work rewards unless separately and explicitly designated.
  - Reading (Q8): summoning rows never enter the attempt projection, so they cannot be linked. No explicit designation path exists in this POC.

## Exclusions

No automatic verifier, no auto-created WorkItem, no arbitrary source URL, no arena or enemy rendering and no Specter-side XP display (FP-10+11), no replay, no store economy, equipment, ranking, time-based XP or loot, no per-user identity, no change to the Specter contracts or tables, no use of `/api/members/*`, no change to the monitoring scope, no new Crew grant.
